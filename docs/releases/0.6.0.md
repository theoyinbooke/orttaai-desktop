# Orttaai 0.6.0

Released November 19, 2026.

Orttaai 0.6.0 brings Express Pro, as Orttaai 1.22 does on the Mac, and makes dictation faster on PCs without an NVIDIA GPU.

## Download

- **Windows**: `Orttaai_0.6.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`)
- **Linux**: `.deb` (Ubuntu, Debian), `.rpm` (Fedora) or `.AppImage`

Existing installs update themselves from the sidebar button.

## Express Pro

- **Express Pro** runs Fluid Speech Pro by FluidVoice, the Mac's Express Pro model, through ONNX Runtime on your CPU. Choose it in Model › Express Model. It's a one-time 801 MB download, checked file by file.
- **25 languages.** Express Pro dictates English and Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish and Ukrainian. Choose your language in Settings › Dictation, which now lists them all. With Auto-detect, Express Pro hands over to Whisper, as on the Mac; Parakeet v3 still handles Auto-detect if you use it.
- For English, Fluid Speech Mini stays the recommended model: on the same recordings it's a little more accurate and faster on a CPU.
- **Word Boost and number formatting run on English dictation only.** Before, they also ran on other languages with Parakeet v3, where they could swap in English dictionary words.

## Faster on Intel PCs

- **Windows no longer slows Orttaai down in the background.** You dictate while another app is in front, which Windows 11 treated as background work and could move to its slower cores. Orttaai now asks Windows not to.
- **Speech uses your fast cores.** On recent Intel chips with fast and efficient cores, Whisper and Express used up to eight threads, mostly on the efficient cores, and waited on the slowest. They now use the fast cores (at least four where the CPU has four). Settings › Model › Decode Threads still overrides it.
- Word Boost no longer runs with as many threads as Express at the same time, so the two don't crowd the same cores.

## Fixes

- Switching Express models: if the new model can't load, Express keeps the previous one instead of falling back to Whisper. Only one Express download runs at a time, and picking the model you're using calls off a switch still downloading.
- Changing between two Express languages no longer reloads the model.
- An Express model in use, or still downloading, can't be deleted.
- Long dropdowns, such as the dictation languages, scroll instead of closing, and follow the arrow keys.
- Setup and the language hint follow the Express model you chose.
