# Orttaai 0.8.0

Released November 25, 2026.

Orttaai 0.8.0 makes dictation much quicker on everyday Intel laptops, and your fixes now teach Orttaai how your words sound.

## Download

- **Windows**: `Orttaai_0.8.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`)
- **Linux**: `.deb` (Ubuntu, Debian), `.rpm` (Fedora) or `.AppImage`

Existing installs update themselves from the sidebar button.

## Faster on Intel laptops

- **No more waiting behind an outdated decode.** Orttaai starts decoding when you pause. If you keep talking, that decode is now stopped at once instead of holding up the one that counts. A fan or room noise no longer stops the pause-time decode from being used.
- **Decode while I speak works with Word Boost.** Each stretch of speech is decoded at your pauses, with a few seconds either side, and the stretches are joined where they agree. Word Boost then checks the whole recording, so dictionary names stay right. Long dictations leave only the last few seconds to decode when you let go.
- **The speed test does more.** It now times dictation with Word Boost, and on Fluid Speech Mini it also tries faster integer maths and keeps it only when it's clearly faster on your PC. It's offered again if Decode Threads is set above your computer's fast cores, and Model › Performance explains why.
- **Word Boost shares the cores better.** On computers with no spare cores it runs right after the speech model instead of competing with it.

## Fixes learn how your words sound

- When you correct a word the speech model wrote (for example a product name it hears as an everyday word), Orttaai learns how your word sounds. Next time, saying your word gives your word, and saying the everyday word leaves it alone. It needs Express in English with Word Boost on.

## Fixes

- Voice editing keeps the spacing around a selection and never edits on half an instruction.
