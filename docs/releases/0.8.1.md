# Orttaai 0.8.1

Released November 25, 2026.

Orttaai 0.8.1 puts your text in place right away, even in Electron apps.

## Download

- **Windows**: `Orttaai_0.8.1_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`)
- **Linux**: `.deb` (Ubuntu, Debian), `.rpm` (Fedora) or `.AppImage`

Existing installs update themselves from the sidebar button.

## Fixes

- **No more pauses before the text appears in Claude, VS Code, Teams and other Electron apps.** Before pasting, Orttaai checks that you aren't in a password field. Electron apps took 5 to 7 seconds to answer that the first time, and again after a while. Orttaai now asks when you start talking, so the answer is ready when you let go.
- **No more processing estimate.** The pill showed a guess like "~120s to process" based on the recording's length, while decoding at your pauses finishes long dictations in a second or two. It now just says Processing…, and the setting is gone.
