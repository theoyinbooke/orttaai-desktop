Orttaai 0.4.0 links your Windows or Linux PC with Orttaai on your Mac, and brings the Mac's Express speech model to the PC.

## Download

- **Windows**: `Orttaai_0.4.0_x64-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`)
- **Linux**: `.deb` (Ubuntu, Debian), `.rpm` (Fedora) or `.AppImage`

Existing installs update themselves from the sidebar button.

## Orttaai Link

- **Link this PC with your Mac** in the new **Devices** page: pick the Mac found on your Wi-Fi and type the code it shows, or paste the link the Mac copies for a PC away from home.
- **Everything syncs both ways**: dictations, Words, snippets, learning suggestions and writing insights. The Mac passes them on to iCloud, so they reach your iPhone too.
- **Away from home** it reaches the Mac through the Tailscale app, and anything said while the Mac is off waits and goes when it's back.
- **Where you said it**: History shows which device each dictation came from (Claude Code on your Mac, Notes on your iPhone) and filters by device.
- **One name per app**: Top Apps, Words by App and writing insights count Chrome and Google Chrome as one app on every device.
- The Devices page shows the Mac, Up to date or what's waiting to send, Wi-Fi or Tailscale, whether iCloud is on, and every device's dictations, words and time.

Needs Orttaai 1.20 on the Mac. Your data goes straight to your Mac over an encrypted connection that trusts only your Mac's key; nothing passes through a server of ours.

## Express: Fluid Speech Mini

- **Express now runs Fluid Speech Mini**, the model the Mac's Express engine uses (by FluidVoice, built on NVIDIA Parakeet). On our real-voice tests with Word Boost it makes about a sixth fewer errors than before, and less than half as many with names.
- It's a smaller download (300 MB, down from 482 MB) and handles other voices in the room better.
- **Already using Express?** Settings › Model offers the switch in one click; the old model is removed afterwards to free its space.
- Other languages keep NVIDIA Parakeet v3.

## Fixes

- **Windows**: Tap Right Ctrl works inside Orttaai's own window, and dictating into Setup's Quick Test or Chat AI inserts the text.
- **Windows**: Store apps like Sticky Notes are named for themselves instead of ApplicationFrameHost.
